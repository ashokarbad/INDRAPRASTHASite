Our Story

A little piece of Delhi,
in the heart of Baner

Indraprastha — the ancient name for Delhi — is our tribute to the city's lanes, its dhabas and its home kitchens, where food is cooked slowly and shared generously.

Brought to you by the team behind Pankti Foods & Caterers, every plate here follows one simple promise: ghar ka swaad, apno ke saath — the taste of home, enjoyed with the people you love. From a lazy morning plate of chole bhature to a late dinner of dal makhani and butter naan, we keep the recipes honest and the welcome warm.

Pull up a chair in our courtyard, where strings of filament lights hang from a living tree, and stay as long as you like.

  • Homestyle Recipes

    Slow-cooked the way Delhi homes cook — no shortcuts.

  • Courtyard Dining

    Dine under a lantern-lit tree, from breakfast to late night.

  • Gatherings & Catering

    Family get-togethers and events by Pankti Foods & Caterers.
